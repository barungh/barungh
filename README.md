## 🧭 Who I Am

I'm a **systems thinker** based in Siliguri, West Bengal — I design backends, databases, and service architectures, then teach others how to build them from first principles.

By day I run **Sunita Enterprises**, an IT solutions shop. By night I build courses, tools, and systems — often in the terminal, always on Linux.

## 📐 How I Think About Systems

> I'd rather spend 2 hours designing the schema correctly  
> than 20 hours migrating it after the fact.

I approach every project by asking:
1. **What does the data look like?** — entities, relationships, constraints
2. **Where are the failure points?** — what breaks under load, concurrency, or bad input
3. **What are the service boundaries?** — what belongs together, what should be separate
4. **How will this be maintained in a year?** — by someone who wasn't there when it was built
